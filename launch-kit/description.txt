Routefold is AI multichain expansion intelligence for onchain products.

Paste your product and receive a transparent, chain-by-chain expansion blueprint
covering ecosystem fit, architecture, risks, and execution.

Routefold builds a Multichain Digital Twin of your product — an explicit,
editable model of its architecture, users, liquidity requirements, transaction
shape, security posture and constraints — then scores every ecosystem against it
with a deterministic engine whose every term is published.

The language model does not choose the number. It explains the engine's output,
identifies trade-offs and unknowns, and may adjust any score by at most five
points, with a written justification, shown separately from the base score.

Where the model is uncertain, it says so: missing data is recorded, confidence is
reported as its own value, and candidates that are too close to separate are
reported as tied rather than ranked into a false winner.
