ROUTEFOLD — FEATURE SUMMARY

MULTICHAIN DIGITAL TWIN
A structured, editable model of your product built from your public sources and
your own answers. Every field is tagged with its provenance — found in a source,
entered by you, or inferred — so you can correct the inferences before anything
is scored.

TRANSPARENT CHAIN SCORECARD
100 points across 5 categories and 17 documented sub-factors. Every row expands
to the full factor table with the points awarded and the reason recorded. Hard
constraints zero a score outright with the reason attached.

BOUNDED MODEL INFLUENCE
The score comes from a deterministic function. The model may adjust a total by at
most ±5 points, only with a written justification, stored and displayed
separately from the base score.

EXPANSION MAP
An interactive graph of the decision: current deployments, the recommended route,
secondary candidates and everything ruled out — with the reason attached to the
node.

CHAIN COMPARISON
Two to four chains side by side: category shape, factor-level points, and the
underlying knowledge-base characteristics that explain the difference.

ARCHITECTURE BRIEF
A deployment model with its reasoning, a component diagram, and specific
positions on token model, messaging, state synchronisation, liquidity, indexing
and monitoring. Assumptions are listed separately.

RISK REGISTER
Filterable by category and severity. Probability, impact, an owner role, and a
mitigation someone can complete. Compliance items are framed as questions for
qualified counsel, never as conclusions.

30-DAY PLAN
Four weeks with milestones, tasks across engineering, product, ecosystem and
operations tracks, dependencies between them, and acceptance criteria that are
objectively checkable.

TECHNICAL BRIEF
An engineering handoff with copyable sections and a complete JSON export.

SOURCES AND ASSUMPTIONS
What was read, when, what could not be read, what was assumed, what is missing,
and the methodology version used.

SHARING AND EXPORT
Revocable read-only share links with 256 bits of entropy. PDF and JSON export.
Share pages never expose account identity or any other project.

PUBLISHED METHODOLOGY
Every category, sub-factor, hard constraint and penalty is documented at
/methodology, with a whitepaper at /whitepaper.
